# Wi-Fi and Radio Frequencies

- The **lower** the frequency, the further the waves travel. The **higher** the frequency, the more information they can transmit.
- 4G mobile has better reception in the countryside than 5G (higher frequency, less reach).
- Wi-Fi signals in the 2.4 GHz band travel further than signals in the 5 GHz band.
